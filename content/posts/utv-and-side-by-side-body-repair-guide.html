Southern Nevada has some of the best off-road riding around, from the dunes to the desert trails outside the valley. Side-by-sides are built tough, but rocks, ruts and rollovers still leave their mark.

Check these after a hard ride

  • Cracked or broken body panels and fender flares
  • Bent front or rear bumpers and skid plates
  • Damage to the roll cage, especially after any rollover
  • Loose mounting points and broken clips
  • Lights and mirrors that are cracked or out of position

Never ignore cage damage

The roll cage is there to protect you. If it is bent, cracked or the welds look stressed after a rollover, have it inspected before you ride again. A cage that has already taken a hit may not protect you the same way the next time.

Repair or replace plastics?

Some cracked plastics can be repaired. Others are better replaced, especially if they are badly broken or faded. We will tell you which makes more sense for your machine and your budget.
