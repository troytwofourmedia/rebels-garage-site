An RV is a big investment and a big target. Even careful drivers end up with body damage sooner or later. Here are the most common causes we see and how the repair works.

Common causes of RV damage

  • Low branches and overhangs that scrape the roof and upper corners
  • Tight turns at gas stations and campgrounds that catch poles and posts
  • Backing into a site and clipping a tree, rock or utility box
  • Awning damage from wind
  • Road debris and rock chips on the front cap

Why fast repair matters

RV exteriors are designed to keep water out. A crack or tear in the skin, a damaged seam or a punctured corner can let moisture in, and water damage inside the walls is far more expensive than the original body repair. If you see damage, get it sealed and repaired before the next rain or the next trip.

What RV body repair involves

Depending on the damage, we may repair or replace fiberglass or aluminum panels, rebuild corners and trim, reseal the area and refinish it to match. Larger vehicles need a shop with the room and experience to handle them, which is exactly why we work on RVs, motorhomes and box trucks.
